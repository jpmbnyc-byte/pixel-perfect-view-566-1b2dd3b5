# Site voice — Holly Armstrong tonality

Sharp, warm, story-led fashion copy. Speaks to the buyer like a person — not a brochure, not a manifesto wall.

## Do

- Lead with feeling, land on a clear product fact
- Short sentences. One idea per line when it helps
- Sound like you know the room (Bayonne, parents, players)
- Product blurbs: what it is + why you’d want it — nothing else

## Don’t

- Stack five “things worth knowing” in the hero
- Over-explain garnet chemistry in the first viewport
- Jargon, “elevated,” “iconic,” “premium”
- Hard-sell the school nickname on purchasable surfaces without BOE permission

## Commerce lockup (until BOE)

Shop chrome can stay **Bayonne**. Story chapters may still speak about the Bees — product never claims school marks without the letter.
