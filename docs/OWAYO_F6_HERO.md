### Collar rationale (do not regress)

owayo collars are **sublimated self-fabric**, not knit rib. Tipping on a folded polo edge cannot hold register across the size run. A flat ringer band on a hidden seam can.

Removing the polo placket also removes the vertical seam that previously split the horizontal **BAYONNE** chest band — the band now runs unbroken.
