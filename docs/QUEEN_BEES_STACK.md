## Layer 3 — Coach / AD outreach

Use as email. Do not paste a store link into the first send unless asked.

**Subject:** A crest for the Queen Bees — no charge, no ask

> Coach —
>
> I run a small kit and identity studio out of Bayonne. I've watched the Queen Bees name get used for years without a mark behind it, so I drew one. It's attached, in vector, and it's yours whether or not we ever speak again.
>
> If the program ever wants it on warmups or fan gear, I can quote that. If not, use it anyway.
>
> — JP, No Parade F.C.

The second paragraph is the whole sales motion. One sentence, past the gift, easy to ignore.

---

## What has to stay out of the copy

Three real constraints:

1. **Don't imply an official relationship** — no "official kit of," no district or school logos — until Bayonne BOE signs something. Right now you're a resident who made a gift.
2. **No student names, numbers, or faces** in commercial creative. Coaches and boosters are the audience; the players are not the pitch and not the buyer.
3. **"Queen Bees" and the Bees marks aren't yours to sell.** Design and give freely; the moment money attaches to the mark you need written permission from the district. Your own crest drawing is your IP — the nickname isn't.

Layers 1–3 have nothing to sell. Do not let a CTA creep into the reveal.
